# Week 1 Day 4

## Hallucination

A hallucination occurs when an LLM generates information that is incorrect, unsupported, or invented.

## Why Hallucinations Happen

LLMs learn patterns from training data.
They predict likely responses and may generate incorrect information when they do not know the answer.

## Grounding

Grounding means using trusted information sources to generate answers.

Examples:
- Internal documents
- SOPs
- Knowledge bases
- Product documentation

## Citations

Citations show where the information came from.

Example:
Approval_Guide.pdf Page 12

## Human Review

Business-critical AI outputs should be reviewed by humans before final decisions are made.

## Exercise

### Factual Question

Question:
What is the capital of France?

Answer:
Paris

Observation:
Low uncertainty.

### Ambiguous Question

Question:
What is the best project management tool?

Answer:
Different responses are possible.

Observation:
Higher uncertainty because there is no single correct answer.

## My Understanding

AI can generate incorrect information called hallucinations.
Grounding using trusted sources and citations helps reduce hallucinations.
Human review is important for critical business decisions.
