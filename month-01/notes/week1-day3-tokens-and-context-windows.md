# Week 1 Day 3

## Tokens

Small units of text processed by an LLM.

Examples:
- Words
- Parts of words
- Symbols

## Why Tokens Matter

Models have token limits.

Input, output and conversation history consume tokens.

## Context Window

The amount of information an LLM can process at one time.

Think of it as short-term memory.

## Conversation History

Previous messages are sent along with the current prompt.

This helps the model maintain context.

## Short Prompt vs Detailed Prompt

Short Prompt:
Generic response.

Detailed Prompt:
Specific and higher-quality response.

## My Understanding

Tokens are the units used by LLMs to process text.
The context window determines how much information the model can consider while generating a response.
