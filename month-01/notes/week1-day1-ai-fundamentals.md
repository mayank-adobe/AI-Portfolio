## AI :

AI is the field of building systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence such as reasoning, decision making, understanding language, recognizing patterns, and solving problems. In AI we have different branches eg. if condition then do this 
can be termed as AI and below there are branches :

## Machine learning : It can be told as the subset of AI in which the machine is learning the pattern here instead of just repating the tasks like we show a pattern and the model learned that pattern and apply in future.

## Deep Learning : It can be told as a special type of machine learning in which it uses Neural Networks with many layers eg. Face Recognition , Speech recogntion , Chatgpt , copilot.

## Generative AI : This is the broad term that we are hearing today everywhere now and it does not just classify data it generates new data or you can say content. e.g. Photos , Code , Document , Design.

## LLM (Large Language Model) : These are type of Gen AI in which it is specialise in language as they are trained on hume amount of text.
